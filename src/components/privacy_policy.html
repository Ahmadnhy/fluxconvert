Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2024

1. Introduction

Welcome to FileRefine. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy notice or our practices with regard to your personal information, please contact us.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your data when you use our web-based tools designed for creative professionals and administrators.

2. Information Collection

We believe in data minimization. The core functionality of FileRefine operates locally within your browser whenever possible. However, certain actions may require data transmission:

  • File Data: Files uploaded for processing are temporarily stored on our secure servers only for the duration of the task. They are automatically and permanently deleted immediately after processing is complete.
  • Usage Analytics: We collect anonymized, aggregated telemetry data (such as tool usage frequency and error rates) to improve our services and ensure reliability.
  • Account Information: If you choose to create an account, we collect basic necessary information such as your email address and preferred settings.

3. Data Security

Security is paramount to our utility-focused philosophy. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect the integrity and confidentiality of your information:

  • All data transfers are encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3.
  • Temporary file storage utilizes AES-256 encryption at rest.
  • We employ strict access controls and regular security audits to prevent unauthorized access to our infrastructure.

4. Cookies and Tracking

FileRefine uses minimal cookies essential for the operation of the platform. We do not use third-party tracking cookies for targeted advertising. The cookies we deploy are strictly for:

  • Maintaining your session state during complex multi-step conversions.
  • Storing your UI preferences (e.g., theme settings or preferred default formats) locally on your device.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy notice from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or relevant laws. The updated version will be indicated by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting your information.
